    <p style="text-align: justify">
      I am a PhD student since September 2025 with Andrea and Thamarai Schneiders as part of the AI for Biomedical 
Innovation CDT. We are investigating the way the overexpression of the global transcription factor Ram-A confers 
antimicrobial resistance to the major pathogen Klebsiella pneumoniae. This species is an opportunistic pathogen which 
typically arises through hospital acquired infections. Some hyper-virulent strains are showing increasing trends of 
resistance to many antibiotics, include last-line ones. <br> <br>

Before joining the group, I completed a double MSc in Bioinformatics and Systems Biology at the University of Amsterdam 
and Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. I completed my thesis as a visiting student at Adrian Bird's lab at the University of 
Edinburgh. I also completed a BSc in Evolutionary Biology at the University of Edinburgh. <br> <br>

Aside from research, I enjoy playing football, going to the gym, trying out different sports, wildlife photography, and 
playing video games in my free time.
